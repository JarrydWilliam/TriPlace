# SameVibe Social Reel Production Source of Truth

**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: August 19, 2026  
**Purpose**: Governing creative and production reference for SameVibe AI-generated Instagram Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts short-form videos (Dreamina + CapCut workflows).

---

## 1. Controlling Product Story

SameVibe is an adults-only (18+), group-first product built around communities, group activities, event-attendance groups, and shared interests. It is not a swipe app, one-on-one matching product, dating app, or simple event marketplace.

### Core Positioning & Taglines
- **Primary positioning**: *"You know what you want to do. SameVibe helps you find the people to do it with."*
- **Supporting taglines**: *"Find your people. Live your Vibe."* | *"New city, new possibilities."* | *"Turn shared interests into real plans."*

---

## 2. Default Eight-Scene Story Structure (Dreamina + CapCut)

Every reel tells a simple, relatable emotional story:

1. **Scene 1 — Desire**: Adult character/couple wanting to do a specific real-world activity. Environmental props make the interest obvious without text (hiking pack, running shoes, bicycle, book, car magazine, baseball glove).
2. **Scene 2 — Discovery**: Character discovers relevant activity, event, or community opportunity on phone.
3. **Scene 3 — Social Friction**: Character realizes missing piece: new to city, no people to go with, or hesitates to go alone (mild hesitation, not desperate).
4. **Scene 4 — SameVibe Social Layer**: Character finds a SameVibe community / attendance group. Emotional shift: uncertainty → relief → genuine interest.
5. **Scene 5 — Commitment**: Character gets ready and actually leaves.
6. **Scene 6 — Arrival / First Contact**: Believable first interaction with small adult group in appropriate public setting.
7. **Scene 7 — Real Participation**: Emotional payoff. Character genuinely doing activity with group (running, hiking, book discussion, car show, comedy show).
8. **Scene 8 — SameVibe Brand Ending**: Over-the-shoulder phone composition with background activity, camera pushes into phone, exact SameVibe loading screen + logo + `"this is my vibe"`.

---

## 3. Character & Visual Rules

- **Adults Only**: All principal and supporting characters must clearly be adults (18+).
- **Style**: Polished 3D animated family-film style, warm cinematic lighting, soft shading, detailed hair & clothing.
- **Continuity**: Preserve face, hairstyle, skin tone, clothing colors across scenes.
- **Format**: 9:16 vertical (1080 × 1920), 30 FPS target.

---

## 4. Dreamina Prompting & CapCut Composite Workflow

- **Dreamina Prompt Length**: Target 3,200–3,700 characters (max 4,000 characters).
- **Reference Image Roles**:
  - Reference 1: Main Character / Master Reference
  - Reference 2: SameVibe Phone UI Screenshot
  - Reference 3: Final Over-the-Shoulder Ending Composition
- **CapCut Compositing**: Overlay exact SameVibe app screenshot onto phone screen with rounded-corner mask and keyframe scale/rotation to guarantee 100% brand fidelity.
